Privacy

Dottino is made by Tim (杭州谱兽智能教育科技有限公司). It is built for children and adults, so it collects as little as possible.

What stays on your device

Your daily results, streak, language and note-name settings are stored only in your own browser. We never receive them. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Microphone

In the sight-singing practice, Basso can listen through your microphone to check whether each note is in tune. The sound is analysed inside your browser while you sing and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The microphone is only on while it is your turn to sing, and you can always tap the notes on the keys instead.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address, who the product is for (child, self or students), your language, and the teacher link you arrived from, if any. We use it only to tell you when the ear-training pack launches. Ask us and we delete it.

If you ask for the next Basso Challenge by email, we keep your email address, your score and where you came from (for example TikTok or Instagram). We use it only to tell you about new challenges and when Dottino opens. Ask us and we delete it.

Founding members

If you register as a founding member, we keep your email address, the grade you chose, your language and the teacher link you arrived from, if any. We use it only to keep your founding price and to send one email with a payment link when the plan opens. Ask us and we delete it.

Visit statistics

We count page visits and a few anonymous steps (for example, how many visitors press Play) with GoatCounter, a cookie-free analytics service. It does not identify you or follow you to other sites.

Children

Children can play without giving any personal information. The waitlist is meant for parents, adult learners and teachers.

中文说明

练习成绩、连续天数和设置只保存在你自己的浏览器里，我们收不到。预约或登记创始会员时，我们只保存邮箱、使用对象或所选级别、语言和推荐老师的链接，只用于保留创始价和通知上线，可随时要求删除。访问统计使用不设 cookie 的 GoatCounter，只匿名统计访问量和几个步骤（比如多少人点了播放），不识别个人身份。孩子不需要提供任何个人信息就能练习。视唱练习可以用麦克风检查音准：声音只在你的浏览器里实时分析，不录音、不保存、不上传；只有轮到孩子唱的时候才开麦，也可以改用琴键。
