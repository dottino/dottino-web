Terms and refunds

Dottino (dottino.app) is an interactive ear-training app (software) for music learners, made by Tim and run by Hangzhou Pushou Intelligent Education Technology Co., Ltd. (杭州谱兽智能教育科技有限公司), China. Last updated: 27 October 2026.

What is free

The daily sight-reading game and Week 1 of each aural plan are free. You do not need an account or a payment method to use them.

Aural Pack

Aural Pack · £15 per grade (founding price)

Access to one grade’s full 8-week ear-training plan in the app (exercises built around the ABRSM aural test format), with 6 months of access from the day you buy. One payment. It is not a subscription and it does not renew automatically. Prices include VAT where it applies.

The Aural Pack opens in December 2026. Until then you can register as a founding member: no payment is taken now. Everyone who registers before launch keeps the founding price and gets one email with a payment link when the plan opens.

Dottino is practice software. It does not provide tutoring, examinations or certificates. It is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by ABRSM, and it cannot guarantee an exam result. ABRSM is a trademark of its owner and is used here only to describe the test format the exercises follow.

Refunds

If the Aural Pack is not right for you, email us within 14 days of buying and we will refund you in full, no questions asked. After 14 days we do not refund, unless the plan did not work as described. Payments will be handled by our payment partner, and refunds go back to the card or account you paid with.

Your progress

Practice results are stored in your own browser. Clearing browser data or changing device removes them; we cannot restore them. See the privacy page for what we collect.

Changes

If we change these terms, the new version will be shown on this page with a new date. An Aural Pack you have already bought keeps the terms that applied when you bought it.
