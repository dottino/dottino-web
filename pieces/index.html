Piece Pre-Flight · Prepare before you play

Warm up your ear & hands before bar 1.

Most mistakes on a new piece happen because key signatures, rhythms and listening collide all at once. Put your iPad on the piano music stand for a 4-minute pre-flight check — works with a real acoustic piano, MIDI piano or screen keys.

All warm-ups use public-domain repertoire (composers deceased 70+ years) and original pedagogical exercises. Dottino is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by ABRSM. For teachers · 3-Min Ear Check · dottino.app
