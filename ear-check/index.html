Ear check · Grades 3–5

How far can your ear go?

Six short listening questions, from Grade 3 to Grade 5 level. About 3 minutes. Sound on.

What the ear check covers

Every ABRSM practical exam ends with aural tests at the same grade as the instrument. Many students play well above the level their ear has been trained to, because lessons spend most of the time on pieces. This check gives a quick picture of where the ear is now.

Grade 3 level: hearing whether music is in 2, 3 or 4 time, and spotting whether a phrase changed in pitch or in rhythm when it is played again. Grade 4 level: remembering the shape of a short tune and recognising a two-bar rhythm. Grade 5 level: the same skills with longer, minor-key tunes and rhythms with dotted notes, where the answers differ by just one note or one beat.

The tools behind each answer

After each question you see the tool that helps: counting to the next strong beat, drawing the tune as a shape line, or following a beat grid. These are the same tools Dottino’s lessons use.
